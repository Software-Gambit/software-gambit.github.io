Space Tower: Idle Defense

The orbital bastion defense game blending deep roguelite strategy, workshop upgrades, and customizable starships.

🚀 Key Features

  • 🛡️ Automated Combat: One-thumb vertical gameplay with tactical real-time upgrades.
  • 🛠️ Permanent Workshop: Reinvest coins & gems across runs to master your tech tree.
  • 🐝 Special Starships: Fly the Hornet Hive MK-02 with autonomous kamikaze bee drones.
  • 🎯 Dynamic Missions: 6-hour rotating challenges dynamically tailored to your unlocks.
  • ☁️ Nakama Cloud Sync: Play offline anywhere, backup your progress seamlessly online.
